** StripPrefix GatewayFilter Factory:
app:
  api-prefix: /api/v1

spring:
  cloud:
    gateway:
      routes:
      - id: nameRoot
        uri: https://nameservice
        predicates:
        - Path=${app.api-prefix}/identity/**
        filters:
        - StripPrefix=2
 - StripPrefix: cho phép thêm 2 dải tiền tố trước identity
